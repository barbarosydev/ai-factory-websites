Our Plant-Based Patisserie

Our Patisserie reimagines classic European baking through a 100% Plant-based lens — using cultured oat butter, organic Brandenburg grains, and seasonal orchard fruit instead of animal dairy or eggs.

Everything on our counter is mixed, shaped, laminated, and baked by hand in small batches right here on Kollwitzstraße.
