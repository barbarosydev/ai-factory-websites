Our Roastery

Every week our small-batch Roastery in Kreuzberg roasts seasonal micro-lots sourced through transparent Direct Trade partnerships with family farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

We roast light-to-medium to preserve terroir clarity — bright florals for hand-poured filters and balanced caramel sweetness for espresso classics.
